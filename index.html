Privacy Policy

Did I Lock It? — last updated November 20, 2026.

Did I Lock It? does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • Photos you take are saved only inside the app on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere.
  • The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.
  • The camera is used only when you tap "Snap" to photograph a checkpoint.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of its photos and data.
